Recommendation: A (top "Needs you" tier) as the primary surface, with B's ◆ Needs you (N) header badge + n cycle layered on for keyboard triage. A is the only option that directly answers "what needs me right now" in one place, with enough of the actual ask (permission vs. question) and wait-time to act — or even open and answer — without hunting through projects; the amber-tinted box is the single emphasis device and vanishes cleanly when empty. B complements it for power users who'd rather stay heads-down and work the queue from the keyboard, and it's the surface that survives when the sidebar is hidden. C is the lowest-friction retrofit (no new IA) but never collapses the cross-project answer to a single count and shuffles rows under the cursor. D (collapsed rail) is the right behavior for that mode and should ship as A/B's collapsed-state fallback — not as the standalone answer. Across all four, amber stays strictly needs-you, always glyph-paired (◆), and disappears entirely in the empty state.
