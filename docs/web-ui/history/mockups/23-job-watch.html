E“Watch triggered” notification — no chip, the note is the payloadcurrent vs proposed · desktop

Why it warns today: steeringClassify.ts hardcodes event === "watch" to the warning tone, and NotificationCard renders the producer's echo attrs 1:1 — job_type watch, status watch, output 0, reason after — metadata soup that says nothing. A fired watch is the expected outcome, never something needing a human: per the color-is-attention rule no watch notification earns a tone chip, ever — not the timer, not a match, not even the budget auto-clear. The title names what happened; the note (or the match) is the prose payload; the watch id retreats to the raw disclosure.
