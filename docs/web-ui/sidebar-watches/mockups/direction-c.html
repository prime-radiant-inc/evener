What this direction commits to

  • The summary line leads with the pending fact — 2 watches · next in 4m — because “something will happen in four minutes” is the most time-sensitive thing a session row can say.
  • Watches get a labelled block inside the fold-out (Watches (2)) rather than sitting loose among jobs and subagents.
  • A cadence micro-visual per watch: ticks for fires so far with the next one bright, and a progress track for time-to-next-fire. This is the one place a watch reads at a glance without reading a word.
  • The Activity panel opens with an “Armed” band of cards, then a selected watch's detail, then running work. Watches are the headline, activity is the log.
  • No attention hue. Ticks and tracks are neutral ink; danger stays reserved for runaway drops.

Data it needs

  • Everything A needs, plus a computed next-fire instant and, for repeating timers, the previous fire instant to fill the progress track.
  • Fire-count history (at least the last few fire instants) to draw ticks honestly.
