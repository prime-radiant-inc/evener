What this direction commits to

  • Count on the summary line. One more neutral segment after the job count: 2 subagents working · 1 job running · 2 watches. No new hue, no badge, no third line.
  • Watch rows are one line — clock glyph in the outdented leading gutter (where a session's state dot lives), note as the title, and the next-fire countdown right-aligned where a session shows its age. A standing condition with no predictable fire shows armed instead.
  • Watches precede activity in the panel, in their own small group. Retained activity is what already happened; a watch is what has not.
  • Detail is a field list. The note as a sentence, then every / next / target / fires, then the delivery log. Runaway drops would render as a danger chip.

Data it needs

  • Per session: active watch count (rollup, like activeWorkSummary).
  • Per watch: id, note, target, cadence (every / after / condition), next-fire instant or “armed”, fire count.
  • Per watch detail: delivery accounting and, when nonzero, runaway drops.
