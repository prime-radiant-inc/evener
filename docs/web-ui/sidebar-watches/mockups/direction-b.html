Sidebar watches · design direction B

Scheduled work

A watch is a peer of a job, not a separate species. Everything a session has running or armed is one list, ordered by when it will next do something, with a single right-hand column answering one question: when. Subagents show their age, jobs show their quiet time, watches show their countdown. The Activity panel puts watch rows in the same tree as everything else.

What this direction commits to

  • One list, one ordering: everything running or armed in the fold-out, sorted by when it will next do something. Watches are not quarantined into their own group.
  • One “when” column with one meaning — a job shows its quiet time, a subagent its age, a watch its countdown. A standing condition shows armed, because a countdown would be a fabrication.
  • A schedule chip beside the title (↻15m, ↻30m ×6h, on output) so cadence is readable without opening anything.
  • Ended watches fold away like inactive subagents and completed jobs — the rail stays about what is still live.
  • Danger only for real failure. A healthy watch is neutral ink. Runaway drops is the one state that turns red.

Data it needs

  • Everything A needs, plus a next-fire instant the server can compute for repeating timers.
  • An ended-watch count and rows for the fold.
  • A sort key: soonest next event, conditioned on the watch having a schedule.
