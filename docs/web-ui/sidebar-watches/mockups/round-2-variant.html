Primary vs variant — the one real difference

  • Primary puts running work first and gives watches their own group beneath it, promoting only the abnormal (“Needs a look”). It treats the panel as a triage surface, which is what every reviewer said the sidebar family is for.
  • This variant puts watches first, ordered by severity then name, on the reading that a person opening the Activity panel most often wants “what have I got armed?” It honours the literal ask most directly, at the cost of pushing running work down.
  • It is also the cheaper build. Watches arrive as a parallel payload rendered as a section, so parseEntry, the ActivityRow union, the tree switch, and ActivityRowDetail are untouched; the primary extends all four with a watch row kind.
  • Both share: neutral ink only, a drawn clock glyph, no countdown, no progress track, no TTL claim, ended watches in the panel rather than a third rail fold, and the showsActivity gate change so an idle session holding only a watch still shows its count.
