## Interpretation — this is the note's strongest demonstrated win

Multi-compaction erosion is **real**. Over 6 successive recursive compactions the baseline
summary lost **2 of 4 facts by round 3** and never recovered them; a verbatim re-stamped
note keeps all 4. Critically, the **pattern** of what eroded is the point:

- **Eroded:** the opaque token `DEPLOY-7Q2X-9F3A-K8M2-V5N1` and the specific detail
  `numShards exceeds 16`.
- **Survived:** the semantically-anchored facts (`account_uuid` column rename, version tag
  `v2.14.0-rc3`).

Recursive summarization preferentially drops **opaque IDs and specific numbers** — exactly
the things a summary is worst at compressing and an agent most needs **verbatim** (tokens,
keys, exact thresholds, hashes). Semantically meaningful facts ride along; arbitrary
specifics decay into the nested summaries and vanish.

## Where this lands across all the evals

| Regime | note's value |
|---|---|
| single compaction, clean facts (live) | **none** — baseline keeps everything |
| single compaction, buried facts (controlled multi-needle) | **rescues 54%** (+1.70 judge) |
| **many compactions, long session (controlled erosion)** | **preserves the opaque/specific facts the baseline erodes (2/4 here)** |

**This reframes the "force a note before compaction" recommendation.** The earlier live
single-compaction tests showed no payoff and I recommended against building the mechanism.
But that under-tested the real case: **long sessions with many compactions**, where
recursive summarization steadily erodes opaque/specific facts. There, pinning (or forcing)
a note that re-stamps verbatim every compaction is exactly the fix — and the erosion the
auto-compactor causes is worst for the highest-value, least-recoverable details.

**Revised recommendation:** the note matters most over **session lifetime**, not any single
compaction. A "force a note at compaction" mechanism is worth building **if it targets
long-running sessions** (many compactions), with the honest expectation that on any one
compaction — especially early, with clean facts — it adds little. The 2×-max-response
headroom threshold is the right trigger design for that mechanism.
