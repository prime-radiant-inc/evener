## Interpretation & caveats (hand-authored)

**Headline:** on this corpus, the agent-authored note's *needle-preservation* value
is **zero** — the baseline gpt-5.5 summarizer (arm A) retained the single key fact in
**every one of the 22 cases** (retention 1.00, zero baseline-drop wins). The measurable
win came entirely from the **steering** (`compaction_instructions`): the note+steer arm
(C) scored **5.00** vs the baseline's **4.09** on the judge's "could a fresh agent
continue?" rubric — a **+0.91** gap. The note *alone* (arm B) moved the judge score
**+0.00**: prepending a fact the summary already kept is redundant.

**What this means for the feature:**
- **Steering is the proven win** (+0.91 judge). The agent telling the summarizer what to
  preserve/drop produces a measurably better-rated handoff even when no fact is lost.
  This corroborates shipping the instruction path (already gated by the obedience eval).
- **The note is *insurance*, not a measured win here.** Its premise — "the cheap summary
  might drop your key fact" — did not fire against a strong summarizer on
  moderate-difficulty histories. The note guarantees verbatim survival; this corpus never
  needed that guarantee.

**Why the corpus is too easy (the load-bearing caveat):** each case has exactly **one**
key fact and an *obviously* droppable bulk block. A strong model keeps one salient fact
trivially. The note's insurance value should appear when the baseline actually *drops*
facts — i.e. **multiple** (5–10) interleaved subtle facts, longer/denser histories, or a
**weaker/cheaper** summarizer model. That harder corpus is the natural follow-up.

**Other caveats:**
- **Self-judge bias:** the judge is the same model family (gpt-5.5) that produced the
  steered summaries, so it may favor its own style. A cross-family judge (e.g. Claude)
  would be a stronger check.
- **Coarse signal:** judge scores cluster at 4/5; needle-match is binary on one string.
- **One arm reused:** B reuses A's summary (note prepended), so A→B isolates *only* the
  note's marginal value over an already-good summary — by construction near zero unless A
  drops the fact.

**Bottom line:** this is consistent with the prior-art finding that agent-chosen
compaction has no clean efficacy edge over a strong auto-summary on easy cases. The
serf-specific signal is: **the steering helps; the note's value is an unfalsified
insurance guarantee** that needs a harder corpus (multi-needle / weaker model) and the
live choice eval to pin down.
