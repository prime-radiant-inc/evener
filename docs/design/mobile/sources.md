# Research ledger

Research checked 5 September 2026. Product pages show published intent and marketing imagery; we have not benchmarked these products hands-on. The interpretations below are proposals for Evener, not claims of demonstrated superiority.

| Primary source | Evidence and date | Transfer to investigate | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Linear mobile](https://linear.app/mobile) | Current product page describes compact workflows, inbox triage, composition and document reading. Its native apps use Swift and Kotlin. | Prioritize useful work away from a keyboard; study how a compact screen retains context. | Assuming a screenshot proves interaction quality, or that its implementation proves a React Native capability. |
| [Linear coding sessions on mobile](https://linear.app/changelog/2026-07-30-coding-sessions-on-mobile) | Published 30 July 2026: mobile change review, line references in steering messages and delegated-session visibility. | Treat review and steering as first-class mobile work, not merely a chat viewer. | Copying issue-management structure where Evener has a different domain. |
| [Things features](https://culturedcode.com/things/features/) | Published features include expanding tasks for editing and optional detail fields. The current homepage still serves the dated 2024 collage used in this lookbook. | Progressive disclosure, legibility and grouping with minimal ceremony. | Presenting the historical image as a verified current OS appearance. |
| [Things updates](https://culturedcode.com/things/blog/) | OS 26 design update published September 2025; later functional releases do not establish a new visual system. | Treat changes to OS conventions as considered adaptations. | Chasing materials at the expense of familiar structure. |
| [Bear](https://bear.app/) | Current product page demonstrates rich notes, Markdown and folding. Image version unspecified. | Continuous reading and editing of substantive text. | Assuming a note editor solves streaming, approval or multi-hub state. |
| [Apple materials](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials) | Current published guidance separates Liquid Glass navigation/controls from content, recommends restraint and native components. | Let chrome support content with appropriate native materials. | Glass around every transcript entry. |
| [Material 3 Expressive research](https://design.google/library/expressive-material-design-google-research?pubDate=20250521) | Published May 2025: research into how shape, color, type and component emphasis guide attention. | Deliberately emphasize the next meaningful action. | Treating expressive design as a mandate for uniformly oversized controls or pills. |
| [Android accessibility](https://developer.android.com/design/ui/mobile/guides/foundations/accessibility) | Official guidance: scalable text, contrast and 48 dp touch targets; page last updated May 2023. Stable guidance rather than a new 2026 trend. | Separate visible density from interactive target size; support non-gesture alternatives. | Making small typography the cure for oversized rows. |

## Synthesis

The strongest direction for Evener combines content-first reading, compact work-oriented navigation and selective action emphasis. This is our inference across the sources. It must be tested against Evener’s real activity streams and both operating systems. “Current state of the art” here means recently verified relevant examples and published guidance, not an exhaustive ranking of every mobile application.

The lookbook embeds official remote imagery with credits and source links. Images can change or become unavailable. It includes a local prototype screenshot separately labeled as a rejected baseline; reference imagery is not an Evener mockup.
