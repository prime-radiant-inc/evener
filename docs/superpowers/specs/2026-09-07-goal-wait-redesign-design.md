**Status:** Approved for implementation planning — post adversarial review round 8 (R1: A 7 Important + 4 Minor, B 9 Important + 2 Minor; R2: C 6 Important + 4 Minor, D 6 Important + 6 Minor; R3: E 2 Important + 6 Minor, F 3 Important + 12 Minor; R4: G 2 Important + 6 Minor, H Minor-only; R5: I 2 Important + 2 Minor, J 1 Important + 3 Minor; R6: K 1 Important + 5 Minor, L 1 Important; R7: M 3 Important + 2 Minor, N Minor-only; R8: O Minor-only, P Minor-only — stop condition met: both reviewers Minor-only with design-ready yes; all adjudicated legitimate, no disqualifications; points B, D, F, G, I, K, M). **Date:** 2026-09-07 · **Branch:** `goal-wait-redesign` (base `33049e4ac`) · **Provenance:** 9-strand state-of-art research (harnesses + durable execution + theory + academic), Q&A decisions (all wait sources; full semantic progress; graduated + resumable; clean-slate), Approach-1 approved with sections §§1–7 presented in chat.

> This spec supersedes the Approach-1 chat sketch where they conflict. Every adversarial finding below is closed in-spec; the disposition table in the appendix maps each finding to its fix section.

## Summary

Replace the mutation-count breaker (`NoProgressLimit=3` / `NeverProgressedLimit=6`, the sole automatic stop) with three separated mechanisms: (1) a uniform **wait registry** with a first-class persisted `waiting` state (parked goals burn zero turns and accrue zero stall signal); (2) a **progress ledger** whose stall signature is repetition-of-`(action, observation)` with no state-digest delta, keeping a total non-advancement backstop and the shipped two-tier strictness; (3) orthogonal **spend budgets** (`maxContinuations`, wall-clock `deadline`, total parked-time cap) with explicit defaults. The breaker graduates nudge → park → block with distinct verdicts, quiet goals notify per-stretch, stop-claims verify only when the goal carries conditions, and blocked goals resume via `/goal resume`. All waits are leases with required deadlines, registration validation, atomic fire-consume, and honest loss notices.

## Goals

1. A goal legitimately waiting on any source (timer, job, delegate, approval, file/HTTP/external condition, child session) parks with zero continuation turns and zero stall accrual, and wakes exactly once on fire or expiry.
2. A genuinely stalled goal (repetition without state movement, or total non-advancement past the backstop) is nudged, then parked, then blocked — with the evidence named — without false-blocking read-heavy investigation openings.
3. No goal is unbounded: every park, wake, re-park, and resume path accrues against a budget or a bound.
4. No silent strands: every lost wait, restart, overflow, or budget clear produces an honest, exactly-once notice.
5. Clean-slate store/gate with a truthful one-way v1 migration (terminals stay dropped; seeding preserves remaining-till-block).

## Non-goals

- LLM-critic / PRM stall judging (deferred behind a cost gate; the ledger is syntactic + digest-based only).
- MCP server-push wakes (2026-07-28 transports have no server-initiated requests; external push arrives via held-open stream or the cheap-poller fallback, never as a spec dependency).
- Event-sourced replay runtime (Temporal-shaped full port deferred; leases + `sclock` are the durability story).
- Token-budget accounting beyond turn/deadline/parked-time caps.

## Design

### 1. Store, states, transitions, verdict table

`Goal{objective, status, waits[], ledger, budgets, stop}` in `agent/internal/goal` (replaces `goal.go`).

- Statuses: `active | waiting | blocked | complete`. Invariant: `status==waiting` iff `len(waits)>0` on every read outside the atomic claim step (which holds the locks throughout — no live read observes the interim, so there is no "suspension," only the pre/post states). Waits leave `waits[]` exactly once — at claim, moved into a transient `pendingWake` list (persisted: `[{wait_id, trigger excerpt, fired_at, superseded}]` — a list, carrying all coalesced fires) that the wake turn consumes; `pendingWake` is an explicit input to `decideGoalStep`. Waits are cleared and their timers disarmed on *every* terminal transition (block/complete) as well as retarget/clear — never only on retarget — so no terminal goal projects a stale `waiting_on[]` and no expired wait survives to re-trigger attach-scan after a restart.
- Only `active` folds ledger outcomes and drives continuations. `waiting` skips every fold and arms no ledger/continuation — only the coalesced wait timer (§2).
- `stop{reason, reported}`: `reported` once-gate stays (`TakeTerminalReport` semantics). Verdicts are distinct — never collapsed:
  - `"no progress"` — stall detector (repetition K or total non-advancement backstop).
  - `"budget exhausted"` — `maxContinuations` consumed **or** `maxParkedTotal` exhausted (§5).
  - `"deadline exceeded"` — wall-clock `deadline` passed (runs across waiting, §5).
  - `"waiting lost: <cause>"` — terminal only when a wait is lost *and* the goal cannot continue meaningfully, decided by rule 5 below (lost this turn ∧ no live waits ∧ no advancement since the loss; the cause is persisted). Any other loss produces the non-terminal honest notice + re-drive (§2).
- Retarget (`/goal <new>`) clears waits, cancels their timers, resets ledger, keeps budgets. `/goal clear` clears all.
- Pure decision (restated per review M1): `decideGoalStep(snapshot, turnOutcome, predicateTruth[]) → drive | park | nudge | block`, where `snapshot` is the full persisted `GoalSnapshot` (§7: status, waits, pendingWake, ledger window, budgets, stage, latch, autoReparks, deadlineFinalDelivered, advancementMarkers, loss cause). `predicateTruth[]` is the evaluated truth of each live lease, computed outside the pure function at an explicit evaluation seam (§3). The pure table pins everything except live I/O. `decideGoalStep`'s domain is non-terminal snapshots only; terminals short-circuit before the table (waits cleared + timers disarmed on every terminal, §1; completes never restored, §7). The gate and settle perform attach-scan + deadline-synthetic claim under `goalUpdateMu`+registry **before** `decideGoalStep`, so rule 1 always observes claimable fires (no gate-wins-before-claim interleave). Deadline expiry is one-shot: the synthetic claim sets persisted `deadlineFinalDelivered=true` at claim time, so rule 3 cannot loop final turns; post-terminal late claims are dropped with no notice (the terminal verdict already stands).
- Verdict truth table (ties broken top-to-bottom):
  1. Undelivered `pendingWake` (fired wait, expiry, or deadline-expiry synthetic) → `drive`, terminal-flagged iff any budget is also exceeded, carrying **all** pending fired triggers (a same-tick deadline never swallows a fired result; the block, if any, happens on the next gate). A terminal-flagged drive sets persisted `terminalPending=true`; while latched, the next gate evaluates rules 2/3 plus the §5 consecutive-re-park graduation **before** rule 1 — on breach it blocks with the corresponding verdict and drops fresh `pendingWake` with the honest loss notice (cause persisted), otherwise it clears the latch and proceeds to rule 1. Perpetual refire therefore accrues *and* enforces (no starvation by flapping predicates).
  2. `maxContinuations` consumed or `maxParkedTotal` exhausted → `block "budget exhausted"` (parked-total is an enforced budget with this verdict, not a dead cap).
  3. `deadline` passed → `block "deadline exceeded"`. Deadline expiry first generates a synthetic expiry wake through the same exactly-once claim machinery (rule 1 drives it as the final evaluation turn with labels + pending triggers); the following gate lands here.
  4. Any live (unfired, unexpired) wait → `park`. A park reached with a same-turn loss attaches the §2 notice to the parking decision immediately; the live wait's eventual wake satisfies the re-drive (no deferred "next turn tail" beyond the park).
  5. Wait lost this turn ∧ no live waits ∧ no advancement since the loss → `block "waiting lost: <cause>"` (cause persisted; the decider is this rule, not judgment). Check-before-reset ordering: rule 5 evaluates against pre-reset markers; recording a loss resets `advancementSinceLoss` **after** the check — so a loss with advancement in the pre-reset window notifies + re-drives once (per §2), while a lone loss with no advancement in the pre-reset window blocks immediately with the §2 notice text carried on the terminal report; in both cases a following gate with no live waits and no advancement blocks. §2's "plus re-drive" applies to non-rule-5 losses.
  6. Stall-K reached (two-tier, §4) → `nudge` on first reaching (stage 1), `park` with bounded auto-wait when the stall looks like waiting (stage 2, bounded by §5 re-park count), else `block` with `"no progress"`.
  7. Total non-advancement backstop reached (§4) → same graduation as (6).
  8. Else → `drive`.

### 2. Wait registry and leases

One registry in the store. Kinds: `until_time | until_job | until_delegate | until_approval | until_event(file_modified | http_match | external_label) | until_child`.

- Lease fields (persisted in full, §7): `{wait_id, kind, predicate{target ids, baseline sha/mtime, URL + matcher, approval content key, child session id}, deadline, registered_at (sclock), idempotency_key{(kind, target identity, canonical predicate, deadline)}, fired_epoch (0 = live)}`. Re-register with an identical key dedupes (no new lease; attach-scan still runs); re-register with the same target but a different deadline/predicate **replaces** (old lease removed, its timer disarmed, new lease validated fresh).
- Defaults table: wait `timeout` required, default 10m, cap 24h; max 8 live waits per goal (shares the session's coalesced goal timer, §7 — never 8 independent timers); total parked time per goal capped (default 24h, configurable); consecutive auto-re-park bound (default 3, then graduate to block); park/wake cooldown after attach-scan-true (default 30s) to break tight park/wake cycles.
- Registration: (a) model-declared via `goal_wait(kind, predicate, timeout)`; (b) harness-auto at delegate spawn, supervised-job start, `ask_user` ask time, child-session record creation. Both paths run identical validation.
- Registration validation (fail-closed reject, mirroring `job_watch.go:278-300,529-548`): target must exist and be owned by the session tree (file path inside the session sandbox and stat-able, URL well-formed with explicit timeout, approval content key matching a live ask, child id a known descendant). For job/delegate targets the check accepts **either** a live wake-capable target (running/settling/stopping delegate; running supervised job) **or** a retained-terminal one inside the record-retention window — the latter routes to terminal catch-up (fire immediately with the terminal outcome as the trigger excerpt) instead of parking. Hallucinated targets (`job_999` with no record at all, unowned delegate, nonexistent file, non-pending ask, malformed URL) are rejected with the reason named — never parked. (Approval answers are excluded from catch-up by design: a consumed answer must not refire; only the live-ask path registers.)
- Predicates are queries over durable or re-derivable state only. `until_approval` keys on the approval content key `(header, question)` **plus the ask generation** (the stable turn/ask-call ID of the `ask_user` call — StableTurnID/ToolCallID precedent, never the positional transcript index, which compaction renumbers — minted at ask time and persisted in the lease alongside the content key) — so a dangling wait from an earlier same-text ask cannot validate against a later generation. Multi-ask disambiguation: all matching asks of the bound generation resolving = fire; the lease records which ask call fired. `ask_user` is root-session-only: a child registering on a parent's ask is rejected with the reason named (no cross-session approval waits in this slice). Empty-header keys (bare question text) are accepted but noted in the wake excerpt as ambiguous-by-construction.
- File/HTTP predicates store baselines and matcher in the lease; evaluation policy (valued, in the §5 constants table): conditions are observed on a poll interval owned by the coalesced timer (default 60s, floor 60s like existing timers), which re-arms to `min(earliest wait deadline, goal deadline, projected maxParkedTotal-crossing, next poll due)` (stated once here and referenced from §§7/9 — never the two- or three-way subsets); stated wake-latency bound = poll interval + notification path. HTTP evaluation binds to the session's existing egress policy: deny link-local/loopback by default, same-policy redirects, response-byte cap (default 64KB), per-fetch timeout (default 15s); wake trigger excerpts carry matcher-outcome + bounded snippet only (default 512 chars), never raw bodies. Sandbox containment (symlink-resolving) is re-checked at every evaluation **and** at the wake re-read; escape fails closed to the honest loss notice. Model-controlled predicate/label fields are size-capped at validation (matcher ≤1KB, URL ≤2KB, label ≤256 chars, restricted printable charset for chip-rendered labels); full payloads persist, wire projection carries labels + deadlines only.
- Fire sources: attach-scan (predicate already true at registration/restore/tick), notification (job/delegate/ask/child event), timer tick (deadline or `until_time`). Exactly one fire site consumes: `claimWaitFireLocked` (the `noteConditionFireLocked` analogue, `job_watch.go:1727-1750`) marks `fired_epoch` under `goalUpdateMu` + registry lock before any kick; `idempotency_key` dedupes registration, `fired_epoch` dedupes firing. Crash between consume and kick re-drives on the next turn tail (the consume record survives; the gate sees a consumed-but-undelivered wake and drives it once).
- Wake = exactly one resume turn per claim batch carrying `(wait_id, trigger excerpt, fired_at)`; the turn's first duty is a re-validation read (event is a hint, state is re-read — the inotify rule; reuse `job_watch` attach-scan/terminal-catch-up semantics). Simultaneous multi-wait fire coalesces: all waits firing on one evaluation tick are claimed together and delivered in **one** combined wake turn (one `maxContinuations` charge), with each wait's excerpt attached. Expiry re-drives exactly one evaluation turn and never auto-blocks.
- Lost waits (restart without predicate substrate, or a disarm race where retarget/cancel wins between claim and kick) convert to an honest notice (`"waiting lost: <cause>; re-arm or proceed without the wait"`) delivered at the next turn tail, plus re-drive — never a silent strand, never a terminal lie. (Clear wins the race differently: §3 — no goal, no notice, wake dropped.)

### 3. Gate and lock order

Replaces `armGoalContinuation` / `settleGoalOnIdle` hold logic. Two parts: pure `decideGoalStep` (§1) + mutator that commits under `goalUpdateMu`.

- Predicate evaluation (delegate-controller / job-manager / file-stat / HTTP-match reads) is computed **before** taking `goalUpdateMu` or `s.mu` (the `hasWakePendingDependents` discipline, `session_goal.go:205-210,234-249`). Time comes from `s.sclock()` only.
- Atomic fire step under `goalUpdateMu` + registry lock: `claim fired_epoch → set status active (transient invariant suspension) → decide drive → release → kick outside the lock` (v1 §7 SetGoal/settle mutual exclusion preserved: kicks happen outside the lock; concurrent `goal_cancel_wait`/retarget/clear against a firing wait either wins before claim (no wake) or loses after claim (retarget → wake carries a `superseded:true` mark and drives a single no-op evaluation on the **current** objective with the stale excerpt marked superseded, never the old objective; clear with no goal current → the wake is **dropped**, not driven, consuming no budget)).
- Settle re-checks leases live (the stale-hold philosophy, `session_goal.go:372-379`): a stale park whose predicate is already true or whose dependents drained kicks exactly once via the same claim path.
- Full lock order for the mutator nesting only: `registry → goalUpdateMu`; kicks happen outside all locks. Controller / job-manager / store locks are leaves reachable **only** via the §3-top pre-read path (predicate evaluation before `goalUpdateMu`/`s.mu`) and never under `s.mu` or `goalUpdateMu` — the L69-style `s.mu → controller` nesting is explicitly forbidden, preserving the `hasWakePendingDependents` discipline. Timer callbacks claim-then-wake across the same order; timers are disarmed on clear/retarget/cancel (no post-clear stale fire — the `goalDependentsHeld`-voiding analogue).
- `decideGoalStep` inputs make the §7 pure tables writable: snapshot (status, waits with truth array, ledger window, budgets, stage) × turnOutcome → verdict, per the §1 table.

### 4. Progress ledger (two signals, not one counter)

Each continuation folds `TurnOutcome{actionFingerprint, observationClass, observationHash, stateDigest, mutated}`.

- `actionFingerprint`: `tool + normalized args` (normalization specified per tool in the spec appendix at implementation time: paths canonicalized, volatile flags stripped, timestamps/nonces excluded; raw tool-name-only or raw-args fingerprints are both rejected — the former false-fires on refining greps, the latter is evaded by trivial arg changes).
- `observationClass`: `ok | empty | error | timeout | approval-pending | external-unchanged`. `observationHash` inputs are canonicalized (timestamps, request ids, RNG redacted) over a stated scope; unhashable/noisy observations fall back to class-only comparison (never "novel by default").
- `stateDigest` scope is fixed and stated: job phases + delegate phases + watch conditions + goal wait predicates + worktree file-listing digest (names + sizes + mtimes, not full contents). History-covering digests are forbidden (they always change and would deaden the breaker).
- Live signals: observation-novelty (canonicalized hash unseen in last N=8), state-digest delta, subgoal evidence (**waits' predicates flipping true only** — `goal_expect` conditions are check-on-claim and never feed the ledger mid-episode, so no expect polling exists and the re-park counter cannot be laundered through trivial conditions).
- Stall signature (keeps the shipped two-tier strictness — the I7 regression is explicitly not taken): K consecutive turns with identical `(actionFingerprint, observationClass)` **and** no digest delta, with K=3 after first mutation-or-subgoal-evidence, K=6 while never-advanced (read-heavy openings are never penalized). `mutated` is a first-class input: a mutating turn with a digest delta resets both tiers; a mutating turn with no digest delta (junk write) still accrues repetition.
- Total backstop (the I2 fix): B consecutive non-advancing turns (no novelty, no digest delta, no subgoal evidence — regardless of alternation with rotation period ≤ N=8) with B=12 default also graduates to block. Longer rotations (period > 8, novelty-every-turn) fall back to the `maxContinuations` outer bound (default 200) — same disclosure pattern as junk-write alternation. Period-2 alternation and timestamp-novelty evasion of non-mutating loops therefore cannot run unbounded. (Mutating-but-advancing loops are bounded by `maxContinuations`, not by B — the ledger is honestly syntactic+digest-only per Non-goals; junk-write alternation is a disclosed residual, not a claim.)

### 5. Accrual and budgets (the unbounded-path closure)

- `budgets{maxContinuations (default 200, cap 1000), deadline (default 4h wall-clock from SetGoal, cap 24h), maxParkedTotal (default 24h)}`. All three have shipped values in a constants table (the `goal.go:25-29` precedent) — no valueless mechanism. Layering: `maxParkedTotal` binds only when configured below the deadline (with defaults it is dead code by construction — parked ⊆ elapsed ≤ 4h < 24h); at exact 24h/24h ties rule 2 reports `"budget exhausted"` (continuation spend is the actionable lever; the deadline verdict is reserved for wall-clock expiry with live work remaining). Polled-kind wait timeouts keep sub-60s validity but their effective fire granularity floors at the 60s poll interval (a 5s file wait fires at the next tick — documented, not surprising). The 30s same-predicate cooldown governs registration/restore attach-scan and notification races only — poll ticks (60s floor) never trigger it.
- The wall-clock `deadline` **runs** across waiting (frozen deadlines would let re-registration park forever). Wake, evaluation, nudge, and notification-driven turns all count toward `maxContinuations` (no free turns at expiry rate). Parked time accrues toward `maxParkedTotal`.
- Re-park bound: at most 3 consecutive auto-re-parks (stage-2 auto-waits or model re-registers without intervening advancement); the 4th consecutive stall graduates to `block`. "Same predicate" for the 30s cooldown is keyed by target identity (path / URL / job id / delegate id / approval key / child id), not full payload — successive genuine modifications still re-park after the cooldown, while identical re-registers are throttled. Intervening advancement (digest delta or novelty) resets the consecutive counter. Already-true predicates at registration do not park: attach-scan-true drives the evaluation turn immediately with a 30s cooldown before any same-predicate re-park, closing the tight park/wake loop.
- Deadline expiry outcome is specified: the goal is **not** auto-blocked silently — the gate drives one final evaluation turn carrying `"deadline exceeded"` + the wait labels, delivers the honest notice, and then blocks with verdict `"deadline exceeded"` (distinct from `"no progress"` and `"budget exhausted"`, fixing the I10 collapse). `maxContinuations` exhaustion blocks with `"budget exhausted"`.
- `/goal resume`: from a `"no progress"` block → ledger reset, waits cleared, budgets kept, `autoReparks` reset. From a budget/deadline block → the renewal check runs **at resume time** (before driving): without `--extend` it is rejected naming the exhausted budget; with `--extend <budget> <value>` (clamped to the §5 caps: 1000 continuations / 24h deadline / 24h parked-total) it drives. The renewal check runs whenever **any** budget is exhausted at resume, regardless of the recorded block reason (a stall-block coinciding with a spent budget renews the same way). Resume with `--extend deadline` resets `deadlineFinalDelivered=false` (keyed to the new deadline value), so a second expiry gets its mandated final turn. Resume from `"waiting lost"` follows the no-progress path (ledger reset, waits cleared, budgets kept, persisted cause cleared) with re-arm-or-proceed guidance per §2. Resume therefore never re-blocks without first consuming an explanatory turn or an explicit renewal. The `superseded:true` no-op evaluation after a retarget/clear race evaluates the **current** objective with the stale excerpt marked superseded (never the old objective); if no goal is current (post-clear), the turn is dropped, not driven, and consumes no budget. Re-registering with a different timeout replaces the lease (key includes deadline); `/goal resume --extend` values clamp to caps (1000 continuations / 24h).

### 6. Graduation, watchdog, stop-claim verification

- Stages: (1) steering nudge naming the repetition evidence ("3 turns of `<fingerprint>` with no state change; state your unblock condition or register the wait"); (2) park-until-wake with a bounded auto-wait when the stall looks like waiting (external-unchanged class, pending asks, live-but-unwaited dependents); (3) terminal block with the correct distinct verdict + exactly one steering transcript note (today's breaker-note behavior).
- Quiet-goal watchdog (v2 direction 2, per-stretch not per-window): at most two owner notices per park stretch — park-start plus one half-deadline reminder anchored to the **earliest** live wait deadline (tie → smallest `wait_id`) — reset on wake/activity, plus one for active-but-quiet past the quiet threshold (default 30m, configurable). Per-goal per-24h ceiling: park-start notices fire only for stretches exceeding the quiet threshold (default 30m — a 60s wait costs zero notices); no watchdog notice emits before threshold-crossing, and the half-deadline reminder fires only if it falls after crossing (a 40m-deadline wait's 20m reminder is therefore suppressed when the stretch ends first — counts, tests, and wording agree). At most 4 watchdog notices per goal per 24h across stretches, repeat-digest stretches past the ceiling emit nothing further. Stage-2 bounded auto-parks never emit park-start (coalesced into the stall episode's single notice). Chip `"nearest label"` = earliest deadline (tie → smallest `wait_id`); the chip never implies more than the `waiting_on[]` list it summarizes. Delivery channel is a named `EventGoalWatchdog` announcement (never a model turn — parked goals run zero turns). "Quiet" is defined: no ledger change, no wake, no owner-visible output since stretch start; the wake itself resets the stretch. A 24h park therefore notifies at most twice (park-start + optional single reminder at half-deadline), never ~144 pings.
- `update_goal("complete")` verifies **iff** the goal carries registered conditions (a condition-registration path distinct from waits; minimal v1 shape: `goal_expect(desc, predicate)` or conditions attached at `goal_wait`/`SetGoal` time). `goal_expect` runs the identical §2 registration validation + attach-scan snapshot at registration (hallucinated conditions rejected immediately with the reason named); expect-conditions are evaluated **check-on-claim only** (no continuous ticks — waits' predicates are the continuous subgoal-evidence source). Goals without conditions keep the v1 self-declare + evidence-audit prompt path unchanged (a condition-free goal completes by self-declare). With conditions: the verifier evaluates the named conditions and rejects with the failing condition named; cost scales with the claim (v2 direction 3). The condition-query schema is defined in the implementation plan before any verification code.
- Continuations carry deltas (v2 direction 4): condition flips + new terminal events/output since last evaluation, not full state.

### 7. Persistence, projection, tools, timers, restart

- `schema.GoalSnapshot` v2: `{objective, status, waits[{wait_id, kind, predicate (full payload: target ids, baselines, matcher, approval content key, child id), label, since, deadline, fired_epoch}], pendingWake[{wait_id, trigger excerpt, fired_at, superseded}] (nullable, cleared in the same commit as the wake turn's tail fold — never at delivery, so a crash between kick and fold re-drives a safe fresh evaluation on restore instead of losing the wake), ledgerSummary{entries[{fingerprint, class, hash, digest, advancement}] (last max(N,B)=12, bounded ~1KB), repetition count, tier (K=3/K=6 strictness), stage (graduation stage: none/nudged/auto-parked — restart after a nudge graduates, never re-nudges)}, budgets{maxContinuations, usedContinuations, deadline, parkedTotal, maxParkedTotal (persisted — restores never silently loosen a tightened cap)}, advancementSinceLoss, lossCause (persisted cause for rule-5 `"waiting lost"` verdicts and pending loss notices), stopReason, autoReparks, deadlineFinalDelivered}`. `SessionMeta.Goal` persists it; restore re-validates every predicate immediately (attach-scan at restore). Non-empty `pendingWake` restores as kick-immediately (not via timer): the claim micro-state is written atomically with the snapshot (`fired_epoch` + `pendingWake` in one store mutation), and a restored pending wake drives its turn before any timer arming.
- Terminals `complete` are never restored (keeps the re-emit half of `TestGoalRestoreOnlyActive` green). Terminals `blocked` restore as **dormant-resumable**: load objective, stopReason, budgets, autoReparks, stage, `deadlineFinalDelivered`, ledger window (no waits, no pendingWake — waits were cleared and timers disarmed at block); arm nothing, suppress the terminal report (persisted stop doubles as the no-reemit marker — no duplicate `EventGoalEnded`), project the blocked chip/status, and serve `/goal resume` + renewal from the restored snapshot unchanged. `TestGoalRestoreOnlyActive` updates to blocked-restores-dormant vs complete-drops. Resume with no goal at all (never set, or cleared) errors naming "no blocked goal to resume" — never a literal objective. Migration table (bound-preserving): old `{streak, madeProgressOnce}` → initial ledger window that preserves *remaining-till-block*, not full history: `remaining = (madeProgressOnce ? 3 : 6) - streak`; seed `K - remaining` synthetic identical non-advancing entries with a dedicated `migrated` fingerprint (distinct from all real fingerprints), so a streak-5 goal reaches K after exactly 1 more non-advancing turn (nudging per graduation, not blocking), and a streak-0 goal gets the full new-tier allowance. Disclosed residual: a post-migration *different-but-still-non-advancing* first turn resets repetition to 1, granting at most K−1 extra turns once per migration (the B=12 backstop still bites). Budgets backfill explicitly (old snapshots predate budgets): `maxContinuations` ← default, `usedContinuations` ← old `Iterations`, `deadline` ← `max(CreatedAt + 4h, restore_time + 1h)` (documented anchor — never instant-expiry, never a fresh 4h laundering an old goal), `parkedTotal` ← 0, `autoReparks` ← 0, `pendingWake` ← empty, `deadlineFinalDelivered` ← false. The migrated near-bound goal graduates, not blocks: streak-5 → 1 more non-advancing turn to *nudge* (stage 1), block only after graduation. One-way, tested — including old-snapshot-without-budgets inputs.
- Restored active goals stay "loaded but idle" (`session_state.go:327-329`); restored `waiting` goals re-arm one coalesced `sclock` timer per the §2 four-way `min()` (never 8 independent timers, never deadline- or cap-blind) and deliver pending loss notices at the next turn tail. Restarted-away predicate substrate (job gone, ask set rebuilt without the content key) yields the honest loss notice, never a silent strand, never a duplicate terminal report.
- Appwire: `EvenerThread.Goal` gains `waiting_on[] + nearest_deadline + progress{usedContinuations, maxContinuations}` (machine-readable, `LangGraph-interrupted`-shaped); `GoalStateData` gains the wait list (fixing the M2 wire gap); new `EventGoalWaiting / EventGoalResumed / EventGoalWatchdog` project to announcements; terminal report stays on every stop path. Suppressed stage-2 auto-park notices still **emit** `EventGoalWaiting` (audit trail preserved for replay consumers) but project silently (no announcement) — emit-vs-project is pinned per event, never ambiguous. Parked-state surfacing: chip aggregates to `"waiting on <n> · <nearest label> · <deadline>"`; `WireState`/autonomy treatment marks timer-parked goals as autonomy-in-flight (never "needs you" with nothing to answer); the notifying turn for a waited target **is** the wake turn (no double-turn accounting).

### 8. Child sessions

The child gap is closed by mechanism, not assertion: a child persists its wait record in its own store; the parent→child forward is specified — parent notification matching a child's `until_child` or a forwarded predicate re-checks the named child's waits under the §3 lock order (child registry → child goalUpdateMu; parent locks never held across the child claim) and drives the child's wake turn through the existing delegate-drive seam (`driveChildIfNotStopGated` path). Stop/drain gates are checked **before** claim (never consume what cannot be driven); a gated forward emits the honest loss notice to the child's next turn tail instead of claiming. `until_child` fires **only** on the child's terminal report (completion/terminal notification), never on intermediate chatter — the matching rule is terminal-only, so wake accounting is deterministic. Cross-session predicate reads (child querying parent job/delegate state) take parent controller/job-manager read locks as §3-top pre-reads (acquire, read, release — never held across the child claim); the resulting match→claim TOCTOU resolves via `fired_epoch` dedupe (double-claim collapses to one wake) or the loss notice (substrate gone). If the forward cannot be built in the first slice, `until_child` from a child is rejected at registration with "child waits scoped out in this slice" — an honest, tested boundary, not a silent strand.
