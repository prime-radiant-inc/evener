**Severity: low** — `appwire-client/typescript/state/mutation/submission.test.ts:97`

The test title is "reports the draft as unchanged and uncleared when the stored draft has moved on since the submission started", but the body asserts `{ clearedDraft: false, draftUnchanged: false }`. "unchanged" and `draftUnchanged: false` directly contradict each other, and the scenario (revision moved from 0 to 7) is precisely the "draft changed" case. A maintainer reading the title could conclude the contract is `draftUnchanged: true` here and "correct" the assertion in the wrong direction. Suggested fix: rename to "reports the draft as changed and uncleared when the stored draft has moved on since the submission started".
