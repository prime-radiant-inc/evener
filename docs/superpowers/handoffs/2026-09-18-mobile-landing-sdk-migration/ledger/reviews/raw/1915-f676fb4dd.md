## Review Findings

- **Severity**: Medium  
  **Location**: `mobile-native/src/PluginsScreen.tsx:128-135`, `mobile-native/src/HubSettingsScreen.tsx:161-165`  
  **Problem**: Keeping these screens mounted across a reconnect prevents their stores from being recreated or refreshed. Plugin/marketplace stores are never notified via `connectionChanged`, and hub settings only refreshes on focus, so data can remain stale—or stuck in a reconnect-time error—after the connection becomes ready.  
  **Fix**: Trigger store recovery on the ready transition; wire extension stores through `connectionChanged`, and refresh/reconcile the hub settings store when reconnect completes.

---

- **Severity**: Medium  
  **Location**: `mobile-native/src/ProvidersScreen.tsx:212-220`, `303-308`, `398-490`  
  **Problem**: Most provider operations remain enabled while the connection is reconnecting or closed. Only OAuth sign-in was gated with `!ready`; provider creation/editing, credential testing/saving, default changes, logout, clearing credentials, and removal can still invoke the client and immediately fail. The credential editor also clears entered input before such a failed request.  
  **Fix**: Propagate readiness to every provider mutation/probe control and guard the handlers as well as the visual disabled state.

---

- **Severity**: Medium  
  **Location**: `mobile-native/src/PluginsScreen.tsx:201-211`; `mobile-native/src/MarketplaceBrowser.tsx:161-293,489-496`  
  **Problem**: `MarketplaceBrowser` receives no readiness state, so marketplace refreshes, retries, add/remove operations, catalog operations, and plugin installation remain enabled behind the reconnect banner. `runGatedMutation` only serializes mutations; it does not verify connection readiness.  
  **Fix**: Pass `ready` into the browser and gate all marketplace reads, mutations, and modal submission controls.

## Summary

The change adds fatal-aware connection banners and retained screen content, but reconnect recovery and readiness gating are incomplete across settings, provider, and marketplace workflows.

## Review Findings
- **Severity**: High
- **Location**: `mobile-native/src/connectionDisplay.ts:46-48,57-60`, used in `mobile-native/src/PluginsScreen.tsx`, `mobile-native/src/HubSettingsScreen.tsx`
- **Problem**: `everReady` and `lastClient` are per-mount refs not scoped to the hub, but the hooks run before the `activeProfile.id !== route.params.hubId` early return. While a screen for hub A shows “no longer selected” after switching to hub B, B’s `ready` state and B’s `client` overwrite those refs, so switching back to A during its reconnect can render B’s client behind a `banner` under A’s identity.
- **Fix**: Scope both hooks by hub (e.g. pass `hubId` and reset the refs when it changes) or key the screen instance by hub so another hub’s connection never populates them.
---
- **Severity**: Medium
- **Location**: `mobile-native/src/ProvidersScreen.tsx:213,308,371,386,418,439,447,455,466,478,490`
- **Problem**: Only the OAuth action and pull-to-refresh check `ready`; the remaining mutation affordances (add/edit instance, set/replace key/JSON, test, clear, logout, remove, make-default, editor) check only `busy`/`writesRefused`, which reflect the last listing not the transport. During a non-fatal flap (`banner`) they stay enabled and issue requests AppWire rejects when not `ready`, unlike `PluginsScreen`/`HubSettingsScreen` which gate everything.
- **Fix**: Include `!ready` in those `disabled` props and guard the corresponding `onPress` handlers as the refresh path already does.
---
- **Severity**: Low
- **Location**: `mobile-native/src/PluginsScreen.tsx:240-246`, `mobile-native/src/HubSettingsScreen.tsx:195-210,228-233`
- **Problem**: Error-retry and upgrade-start handlers rely solely on `disabled={!ready}` while the adjacent `onRefresh` handlers also guard with `if (ready)`. A missed `disabled` or programmatic press issues a request while disconnected.
- **Fix**: Add the same `if (ready)` guard inside those `onPress` handlers before calling `fetchPlugins`, `refresh`, or `upgrade.start`.
## Summary
Survives connection flaps behind a banner with fatal-aware walls and ready-gated mutations, but leaks cross-hub client state and leaves most Providers mutations ungated.
