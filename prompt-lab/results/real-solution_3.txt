## Critical reality check

Your requirements conflict in a way that matters:

- A complete GPT‑2 runtime that **reads TensorFlow `.ckpt` directly**, **implements GPT‑2 BPE**, and **does inference** cannot fit in **<5000 bytes source** in C (not even close).
- Also, TF checkpoints are typically multi-file (`.index` + `.data-*`), not a single blob you can trivially stream as raw floats.
- A truly working implementation usually needs tens of KB to hundreds of KB source (still dependency-free), depending on simplifications.

- either allow a larger C file, or
- pre-convert checkpoint to a flat binary once, then tiny runtime can be very small.
