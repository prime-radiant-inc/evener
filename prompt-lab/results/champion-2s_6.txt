   Correct GPT‑2 tokenization is itself nontrivial (byte-to-unicode mapping, pair ranks, merges).

   A real, correct loader + tokenizer + forward pass for GPT‑2 124M in plain C cannot fit in 5KB source while still being readable/maintainable and compilable with only `gcc -O3 -lm`.
