Event Tape — a per-turn glyph ribbon

Every turn of a 263-turn session becomes one column in a fit-to-width ribbon. Column hue = turn kind, inner bar height = tool-call intensity, compaction = amber divider, failure = red corner glyph, steering = amber dot. Bursts, stalls, failure clusters, and compaction boundaries become patterns you read before clicking anything. Delegate sessions and background jobs are separate lanes beneath.
