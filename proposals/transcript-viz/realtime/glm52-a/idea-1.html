What you see at a glance, live: as turns stream in from the right, the tape fills column by column; older columns compress left to keep the full-session-so-far pattern on one screen. A green-glow border marks the live cell. The streaming ticker narrates each event as it arrives (steering, compaction, tool_results, failure). Live questions answered: what pattern is forming right now (burst, stall, retry loop)? what just changed? is current activity normal for this session? Bursts show as rapid column arrivals; stalls as gaps; retry loops as a red glyph followed by rapid tool_results; compaction as an amber full-height divider. The delegate lanes beneath grow right-ward as new children spawn; the top lane's ticks mark background jobs, with a pink tick for a detached job that outlives the session.
