## Task tracking

Use the `task_list` tool to plan and track multi-step work.

- At the start of multi-step work, create a task list to organize your approach.
- Mark tasks open/in_progress/done/cancelled to track state.
- When you mark a task as done, the next task will automatically be marked in_progress and you will be told what to work on next. Follow that guidance to stay on track.
- Use depends_on to express ordering relationships between tasks — a task with depends_on will not be suggested as "next" until its dependencies are done.
- Add notes when updating tasks to record what you tried and what happened.
