## Blocker (kata filed)

`isolation=worktree` delegates are bwrap-confined even under parent
`--sandbox off`, and the lane's backing gitdir
(`<repo>/.git/worktrees/<dlg_…>`) is not mapped writable — a sandboxed
delegate cannot `git commit` in its own lane (exit 128, read-only
`index.lock`). Reproduced 3× deterministically with mount-count controls;
analysis in the gpt-5.4-mini lane report. **Pre-existing on main** — this
branch touches neither `agent/sandbox/` nor `agent/execenv/`. Strong models
masked it by committing on the `dlg_` branch from the unconfined parent side,
which is why the gpt-5.5/kimi lanes completed. CI misses it because
`dld_e2e_test.go` commits lane work on the host (`laneCommit`), never through
a sandboxed delegate. Kata filed with repro + suggested fix (map the backing
gitdir + common object store writable, mirroring reroot.go's ControlPolicy).
